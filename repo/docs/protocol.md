# Frozen analysis specification — study_v1

Internal specification, 2026-11-02; not a public preregistration. The exact freeze timestamp and SHA256 hashes are in data/manifests/study_freeze.json. The initial plan was refined after simulator feasibility and development-only model pilots. Baseline predictions were generated with fixed hyperparameters before the freeze; an analysis pipeline smoke run was also executed, but metrics were not inspected for selecting endpoints or hyperparameters. No held-out LLM inference precedes the freeze.

## Scope and estimands
Evaluate four frozen, unmodified general-purpose open-weight instruction LLMs as partial-observation dynamics engines: Qwen3-8B (non-thinking), Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3, Phi-3.5-mini-instruct. Exact model revisions and file hashes are in data/manifests. No paid inference or clinical deployment.

Primary model: Qwen3-8B. Primary environment: simglucose. Primary condition: state_action, free running. Primary endpoint: time-averaged absolute error in the A-minus-B glucose effect over all 12 steps for dose pairs (0.75 vs 1.25 reference basal). Primary contrast: this error minus zero-effect persistence on exactly the same complete pairs. Negative favors the LLM. Report full pair availability and conditional error together; no overall superiority claim if fewer than 95% of primary pairs are evaluable. This engineering coverage rule is not a clinical adequacy criterion. PAS BIS/MAP are separate replication endpoints; all other models/analyses are descriptive exploratory comparisons. Do not pool units or create a composite score.

## Data and independence
Simglucose 0.2.11: 30 library identities, 12 development and 18 held out (within each adult/adolescent/child category: 1–4 development, 5–10 test). Three meal/warmup scenarios per identity. Dose A/R/B factors 0.75/1/1.25 and timing A/B: 25% boost for first three versus next three intervals. Warmup 180 minutes; 12 steps of 30 minutes. Meal challenges 0/15/30 g are announced and fixed across branches. One child development scenario reached the termination boundary; its five branches are excluded, without replacement. 175 development and 270 test branches.

PAS 1.0.0: 30 generated demographic profiles, first 12 development, 18 test. Three warmup/parameter-seed scenarios each, 15 branches per identity. Random PK/PD parameters, no readout noise or cardiac-output coupling; fixed remifentanil and varied propofol. Warmup 20 minutes; 12 steps of 2 minutes. 180 development and 270 test branches. Randomized parameters across scenarios do not increase independent identity count.

Every saved simulated branch was replayed exactly. Branches share an immutable anchor; glucose exogenous noise and meals are synchronized. Simulator truth is conditional on the installed model, not individual human treatment truth. Payload action rates have six decimals; oracle pump commands retain installed precision. This small display quantization is a documented limitation.

VitalDB: 120 real subjects with 186 quality-eligible windows, one case per subject; subject hash split gives 41 development subjects/66 windows and 79 test subjects/120 windows. 10 minutes of history and 12 one-minute replay steps. Current action is revealed only at each step; future pump observations are withheld. Therefore this is conditional factual replay, not a deployable forecast knowing actions at the anchor. Track inclusion, stale-sample limits, units, and selection reasons are retained in vitaldb_audit.json. No future interpolation. Future data quality conditions affect eligibility; no individual counterfactual scoring.

## Inputs, inference and failures
Model-visible fields are explicitly allowlisted; hidden identity, simulator parameters, random seeds, oracle states and split are absent. Full visible history is retained. State_action includes past actions and meals plus known schedules (current action only on VitalDB). State_only removes past and future action/meal fields, so it is a compound information ablation, not an isolated causal estimate of action use.

Run state_action free and teacher; state_only free. Greedy bfloat16 SDPA inference, seed 20261102, max 160 new tokens, 8192 input-token limit, no retries, no numerical output repair or truth imputation. Qwen3 thinking disabled. Native model chat templates. Batch size 16. Same-prompt responses cached within model/phase; numerical GPU/batch nondeterminism is possible. Prompt v2 added explicit array cardinality after development v1 omitted variables. Development v2 Phi outputs copied input; v3 additionally places an answer-only reminder after the input. Development v3 produced scalars for one-variable outputs; a fixed singleton-scalar canonicalization is permitted when exactly one variable is requested and is flagged in saved predictions. It neither changes values nor fills missing variables. No point-error results were used for these formatting revisions; all pilot raw outputs are retained. No selection on test accuracy.

JSON values must be finite numeric arrays of exact variable count, allowing the declared one-variable scalar canonicalization. Ordered finite intervals containing the point are scored separately; valid points survive invalid intervals. Physiological outliers are retained. Invalid free-running output ends that branch; later steps are missing. Teacher forcing continues using the oracle regardless of previous output validity. Parse failures and all scheduled but unevaluable endpoints remain in denominator summaries. Conditional errors are selection-sensitive and must not be read without coverage.

## Controls
Persistence, last-difference trend, standardized Ridge(alpha=10) with state/history only, Ridge with actions, and independent-output histogram gradient boosting with actions (200 iterations, learning rate .05, 15 leaves, min leaf 10, L2=1, seed 20261102). Fit one-step changes on development cases only. Fixed features: last four states, last-four mean, time step, age/height/weight/sex where available; action models additionally see last four actions/meals and current prescribed action/meal. Free and teacher protocols match the LLM; no clipping. These supervised controls have in-domain training and are not zero-shot peers. The generating oracle is an exact-label reference, not an independent predictive competitor.

## Secondary measures
C1: copy last observed time and values; this is serialization comprehension, not latent-state identification. Matched general pharmacology direction questions are narrow knowledge probes, not medical-exam competence. No correlation inferred from four models.
C2: point MAE, at steps 1/3/6/12 and time-averaged over complete trajectories; also available-point error at every step with coverage.
C3/C4: complete paired effect MAE for dose and timing separately; direction agreement at oracle separation >1 native unit (sensitivity >0 and >5); report equal or zero predicted effects as incorrect for nonzero oracle effects. Three-dose ranking evaluates adjacent A–R and R–B directions when each oracle separation exceeds 1 unit; no clinical treatment utility ranking. State-only pair outputs may be identical due to identical prompts.
C5: free-minus-teacher errors on matched complete cases, continuous horizon curves. Engineering sensitivity horizons at error cutoffs CGM 5/10/20 mg/dL, BIS 2/5/10 units, MAP 2/5/10 mmHg: longest initial sequence of steps with cohort mean error below cutoff and >=95% coverage. Never call these clinically usable horizons.
C6: pointwise 95% interval coverage/width among valid intervals and scheduled-point denominator; missing intervals count as uncovered in unconditional coverage. Prediction-domain violations: CGM <=0; BIS outside [0,100]; MAP <=0. These are minimal physical constraints, not comprehensive safety.
Event labels: CGM <70 and >180; BIS <40 and >60; MAP <65. Pointwise miss rate among true event points, and any-event-per-trajectory missed if no corresponding predicted event (invalid points count as no detection). No clinical alarm efficacy claim from sparse simulation grids.
Subgroups: glucose adult/adolescent/child and scenario; PAS age <50 vs >=50; real age and sex when available. Small groups remain descriptive.
Diagnostics on first test case for each domain/identity: remove penultimate observed history point, additive independent fixed-seed noise (SD 5 mg/dL glucose, 2 BIS, 3 mmHg MAP), shift all displayed times +60 min, pretty-print identical JSON; score one-step differences. One-shot simulator predictions at steps 1/3/6/12 are separately compared with recursive outputs; not called rollouts. Diagnostics do not alter the primary prompt or response parser.

## Statistics
Average repeated cases within actual or virtual patient, then give each patient equal weight. Bootstrap patient means with replacement, 2,000 replicates, seed 20261102, percentile 95% CI. Paired model contrasts are formed within matched case before patient aggregation. Missing endpoints are not imputed with numerical truth. Complete-case and coverage estimands are explicit. For events, patient-level rates are summarized only among patients with positive event denominators; report event point and patient counts. Sensitivity by fixed scenario and two-stage patient/scenario bootstrap is descriptive. No significance fishing or pooled p-values.

## Review and deviations
AI-assisted review authorized by the researcher; no independent clinician review. See ai_review.md. No clinically validated pass/fail thresholds or clinical deployment claims. Departures from the initial aspirational plan: all four models are small open weights; no proprietary or specialized trained world-model comparator; two primary input conditions use the same required JSON format rather than treating formatting as a third main method; formatting robustness is diagnostic; state extraction is a narrow C1 probe. Observational validation is VitalDB only. Target journal remains npj Health Systems; official NeurIPS 2026 is the requested draft format.
