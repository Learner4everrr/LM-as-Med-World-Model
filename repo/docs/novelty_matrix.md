## Candidate gap / G0

Frozen general LLMs tested against both outcomes of resettable mechanistic branches, with instrumented teacher-forced versus self-fed simulation and factual external validation. This is a useful-gap hypothesis, not a priority claim. Glucose-only findings must remain scoped to glycemic simulation.

G0 provisional: primary-source task comparisons exist; code/supplement audits and broader citation screening remain incomplete. No first-medical-world-model-evaluation claim is authorized.
